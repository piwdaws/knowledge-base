- Search engine discovery is a reconnaissance technique that uses search engines to discover publicly indexed information about a target. 
- Instead of directly interacting with the target website, the search engine's index can reveal information that may not be obvious from normal browsing.

---
## Search engine discovery can reveal: 
- Publicly indexed pages 
- Login and administrative pages 
- Documents and files 
- Employee or organisational information 
- Technology information 
- Exposed or forgotten content 
- Potentially sensitive information

---
## Search Operators 
- Search engines provide special operators that allow searches to be narrowed to specific domains, URLs, file types, titles, and text. See [[Search Operators]].

---
## Google Dorking 
- Google Dorking uses search operators to perform targeted searches for information that may otherwise be difficult to find. See [[Google Dorking]]
